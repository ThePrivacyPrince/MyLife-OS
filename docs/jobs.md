# Notes and Job Prep

## 📝 Study notes (University → Notes)
- **Write today's notes:** add a title if you like, then pick a course, a unit and a job role (all optional).
- **One note, several places:** a note tied to a unit also appears in that unit's **📝 Notes** tab, and on every job role linked to that unit.
- **#hashtags** in the text become tags. Search, filter by tag or course, and see notes grouped by day.
- **Streak:** "Days in a row" counts days with at least one study note.

## 💼 Job Prep (sidebar → Jobs)
- **Add role:**
  - Paste the posting (postings get taken down).
  - Its bullet points become your **requirements**.
  - Link the University courses that prepare you.
- **🧭 Strategy:** nine sections, each with a prompt: their top 3 needs, one-line pitch, proof points, gaps and how you're closing them, stories, questions to ask, 30-60-90 day plan, company research, logistics.
- **✅ Requirements:** what they want vs what you can prove. Rate each 🟢 strong / 🟡 partial / 🔴 gap / ⚪ not rated, and write the proof.
- **🎓 Study & proof:**
  - Linked courses and units, with their mastery levels.
  - **Suggestions:** units whose flashcard terms appear in the posting (MSA, DPA, redline...).
  - **Proof from study:** evidence from units you've applied.
  - Repos and portfolio pieces.
- **🛠️ Tasks & stories:**
  - Prep tasks are normal LIFE OS tasks with a full audit trail. They show on Today and in the Planner.
  - Link stories from the **Story bank**.
- **📝 Notes:** recruiter calls, research and debriefs, plus your study notes on the linked units.
- **⚙️ Status:** researching → preparing → applied → interviewing → offer → accepted, or closed (a reason is required). Set a **next step** and its date: Today reminds you 7 days ahead.
- **🕓 History:** append-only log of status changes, next steps, strategy edits and links.
- **Story bank:** STAR interview stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result), written once and reused across roles.

## Readiness (evidence only)
- **Requirements proven:** strong = 1, partial = ½, gap or not rated = 0, averaged.
- **Linked study applied:** the share of linked units at 🟢 applied or 🏅 mastered.
- **Readiness:** the average of the two. With nothing to measure, it shows "-", never a made-up number.

## Privacy
Roles, notes and stories live only in your local database (`data/`, git-ignored). Nothing here goes to Git.
